2.1 Principles

All Ruled.

Z0. specdev is a correctness tool, not a security boundary. It reports whether the spec and the proof are in order, and asks the operator at the few points where a person decides. It does not try to stop a determined agent or a hand-made tag; that is trust and team process, outside the toolkit.
